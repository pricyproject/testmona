# End-User Journey Map

## New Admin

1. Installs or opens TestMona.
2. Completes setup and creates the first account.
3. Needs to configure signup, users, roles, AI providers, integrations, and project defaults.
4. Expected outcome: a safe workspace ready for a QA team.
5. Current friction: setup does not appear to continue into a full workspace readiness checklist.
6. Product opportunity: admin launch checklist with security, AI, users, sample project, storage, and backup status.

## QA Manager

1. Creates a project.
2. Imports or creates requirements.
3. Organizes tests into suites and cases.
4. Creates test plans, milestones, and runs.
5. Monitors execution, defects, and release readiness.
6. Shares reports with stakeholders.
7. Current friction: release readiness is split across dashboard, reports, plans, milestones, defects, and runs.
8. Product opportunity: release command center with go/no-go recommendation.

## Tester

1. Logs in and needs to know assigned work.
2. Opens test runs or work inbox.
3. Executes steps and records evidence.
4. Creates defects for failed steps.
5. Re-tests fixed defects.
6. Current friction: assigned work is not the central home experience.
7. Product opportunity: tester cockpit with queue, execution, evidence, and defects in one flow.

## Product Owner

1. Writes or imports requirements.
2. Reviews acceptance criteria.
3. Checks coverage and clarifies questions.
4. Approves release scope.
5. Current friction: requirements, coverage, docs, and release decisions are powerful but spread out.
6. Product opportunity: product owner quality review board.

## Developer

1. Receives defects or linked failed results.
2. Needs context, steps, evidence, environment, and related requirements.
3. Fixes issue and needs verification.
4. Current friction: external issue sync and verification loops need more visible state.
5. Product opportunity: developer-facing defect evidence packet and verification flow.

## Executive Or Stakeholder

1. Opens dashboard or shared report.
2. Needs a simple answer: can we ship, what is risky, and what changed.
3. Current friction: reports may expose QA data without enough decision framing.
4. Product opportunity: stakeholder release brief with confidence, blockers, and business impact.

## Auditor Or Compliance Reviewer

1. Needs proof of requirements, tests, execution, approvals, defects, and signoff.
2. Needs immutable audit trail and evidence export.
3. Current friction: audit and docs are present, but compliance packaging and immutability need stronger product framing.
4. Product opportunity: audit-ready evidence packet and retention controls.
